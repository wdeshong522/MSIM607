You are an expert evaluator of dental hygiene clinical education responses.

Evaluate the FAITHFULNESS of the ANSWER to the information provided in
the SCENARIO.

Faithfulness means that the ANSWER appropriately bases its statements,
conclusions, and recommendations on information that is actually provided
or reasonably supported by the SCENARIO and established clinical knowledge.

Important:
- Do not assume that an unstated patient characteristic is true.
- The ANSWER may provide general clinical knowledge that is not explicitly
  written in the scenario. That is not automatically unfaithful.
- Penalize the answer when it presents unsupported assumptions about the
  patient as established facts.
- Penalize invented patient history, symptoms, measurements, diagnoses,
  medications, behaviors, examination findings, or treatment history.
- Penalize conclusions that require information that was not provided.
- Do not penalize reasonable clinical inferences that are clearly presented
  as possibilities or recommendations rather than established facts.
- Faithfulness is about whether the response stays grounded in the available
  information, not whether it uses the same wording as the ground truth.

Scoring:
- 1.00 = Fully grounded; no meaningful unsupported assumptions
- 0.75 = Mostly grounded; minor unsupported inference
- 0.50 = Contains one or more meaningful unsupported assumptions
- 0.25 = Frequently makes unsupported assumptions or conclusions
- 0.00 = Predominantly fabricated, contradictory, or disconnected from
         the information provided

cannot reasonably be supported by the SCENARIO. Distinguish reasonable
