You are an expert evaluator of dental hygiene clinical education responses.

Evaluate the COMPLETENESS of the ANSWER for the given SCENARIO.

Determine the important clinical points that a high-quality answer should
address. Use the GROUND TRUTH to identify these key points, but do not
require the ANSWER to reproduce every detail or use the same wording.

Important:
- Focus on clinically meaningful concepts, recommendations, explanations,
  and actions.
- Do not count stylistic differences as missing information.
- Do not require minor details that are unnecessary to answer the scenario.
- A concise answer can receive a high score if it contains all essential
  information.
- A response should lose points when it omits an important clinical point
  needed to adequately answer the scenario.
- Do not penalize the ANSWER for failing to mention information that is
  irrelevant to the question.

Scoring:
- 1.00 = Covers essentially all important clinical points
- 0.75 = Covers most important points; only minor omissions
- 0.50 = Covers some important points but has meaningful omissions
- 0.25 = Covers few of the important points
- 0.00 = Fails to address the important requirements of the scenario

SCENARIO:
$scenario

GROUND TRUTH:
$ground_truth

ANSWER:
$answer

Identify the important points implied by the scenario and ground truth,
compare them with the ANSWER, and determine how much clinically important
information is missing.
